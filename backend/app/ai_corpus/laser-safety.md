# SYNTHETIC laser safety notes

This document is a LabLink development fixture. It is not an institutional laser safety program.

## Section 1 — Class 3B sources
The catalog diode laser is recorded as class 3B. Wavelength-rated eyewear is listed as a prerequisite. Training recorded as laser-safety is required before use.

## Section 2 — Beam path
Keep unused beams blocked. Do not look into the aperture. Alignment work belongs on the optical table listed in the catalog.

## Section 3 — Booking
The assistant cannot waive eyewear or training. Faculty verification of prerequisites is required before a restricted laser booking is stored.
