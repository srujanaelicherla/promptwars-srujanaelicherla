# SYNTHETIC fume hood usage notes

This document is a LabLink development fixture. It is not a chemical hygiene plan.

## Section 1 — Status
The catalog chemical fume hood may be recorded as maintenance. Equipment in maintenance is not operational for new bookings.

## Section 2 — Sash and airflow
Keep the sash at the marked height. Confirm face velocity before solvent work. Flammable HPLC waste belongs in labeled containers.

## Section 3 — Access
Chemical hygiene training is listed as a prerequisite. This document does not authorize hood use and does not report whether the hood is free at a given hour.
