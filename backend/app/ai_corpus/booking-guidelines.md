# SYNTHETIC booking guidelines

This document is a LabLink development fixture. It is not a university policy.

## Section 1 — Scope
LabLink bookings are recorded against catalog equipment. Restricted items list safety prerequisites and cannot be booked until a faculty member verifies those prerequisites.

## Section 2 — Slot rules
Bookings use half-open time ranges. Adjacent slots that meet at a boundary do not overlap. A booking must last at least 15 minutes and at most 8 hours.

## Section 3 — Conflicts
When two pending access requests compete for the same equipment and overlapping time, faculty confirmation is required. Ranking scores are advisory and do not book the resource.

## Section 4 — What this document is not
This text does not grant access, approve requests, or report live availability. Live free/busy data comes only from LabLink booking records.
