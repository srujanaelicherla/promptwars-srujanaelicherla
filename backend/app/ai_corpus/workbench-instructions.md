# SYNTHETIC open workbench instructions

This document is a LabLink development fixture. It is not a laboratory SOP.

## Section 1 — Unrestricted bench
The catalog open workbench has no specialized training recorded. It is intended for low-risk layout and electronics bring-up.

## Section 2 — What it is not
The workbench is not a substitute for the CNC mill or for chemical fume hood work. Do not treat this page as live availability.

## Section 3 — Booking
Students may request a DEMO booking on this resource when it is recorded as available and the slot is free in LabLink booking records.
